## ADSB

stands for Amazon Data Science Books, which is the topic of the dataset. You can find the data attached as a csv file. Some information on the data follows:

## Dataset Description

The dataset contains 946 books and 11 variables related to
Data Science, Statistics, Data Analysis, Python, Deep Learning,
and Machine Learning.

| No. | Variable | Description |
|-----|----------|-------------|
| 1 | `price` | Price of the book in US dollars ($). |
| 2 | `pages` | Total number of pages in the book. |
| 3 | `avg_reviews` | Average customer rating (out of 5 stars). |
| 4 | `n_reviews` | Total number of customer reviews for each book. |
| 5 | `star5` | Proportion of 5-star reviews. |
| 6 | `star4` | Proportion of 4-star reviews. |
| 7 | `star3` | Proportion of 3-star reviews. |
| 8 | `star2` | Proportion of 2-star reviews. |
| 9 | `star1` | Proportion of 1-star reviews. |
| 10 | `weight` | Weight of the book (in pounds or ounces). |
| 11 | `language` | Language in which the book is written. |
